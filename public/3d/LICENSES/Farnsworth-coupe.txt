Farnsworth coupe — original project asset, September 2026

The mesh, UV layout, occlusion bake, scene lighting and poster were created
for this project using the reproducible Blender script in
src/components/hero/three/pipeline/build_coupe.py.

No third-party vehicle mesh, scanned vehicle, branded CAD source, badge,
stock photograph or purchased asset was used. No model attribution or
asset subscription is required. These original project assets are provided
under the repository's MIT license; see /LICENSE.

Blender and glTF Transform were production tools, not incorporated assets.
The studio environment is constructed at runtime from geometric softboxes;
no third-party HDRI is downloaded or bundled.

The study is a fictional brand illustration. It is not a repair record,
structural inspection or depiction of an actual inventory vehicle.
